A one-line 1-star review written by a houseplant about its human owner, for make.fun's "One Star, Written in Sap" bounty ($2 in USDC).

**The hook:** the owner waters the plant with LaCroix, so the plant's roots have developed a taste for artisanal sparkling mineral water — and now judge the tap water accordingly. A plant's-eye grievance about water, with a surprising turn: the victim became a snob.

The entry line (131 characters):

> One star: you water me with LaCroix — my roots now expect artisanal sparkling mineral content and judge your tap water accordingly.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out node main.ts
cat /tmp/out/entry.txt
```

`main.ts` runs on Node 22 (stdlib only, no network, no external files). It writes the line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`) and throws unless the line is 1–279 characters and not only whitespace.
